# Organisation, legal form and ownership examples

The [PlantUML diagram](Organization_LegalForm_Ownership.PlantUML) presents Bart's two illustrative cases. The single source contains two standalone diagrams. Renderings: [Jansen SVG](Ownership_Jansen.svg), [Jansen PNG](Ownership_Jansen.png), [Braindex SVG](Ownership_Braindex.svg) and [Braindex PNG](Ownership_Braindex.png). These are conceptual examples, not verified statements about actual register entries. The later consolidated ontology supplies LegalForm, hasLegalForm, EnterpriseOwner and Shareholder; the [RDF example](ownership.ttl) expands the drawings into that pattern.

- **Fietsenservice Jansen** is represented as an organisational enterprise. The two unnamed sons are human Agents fulfilling co-owner roles towards that enterprise. The legal form is the KVK variant of a sole proprietorship with multiple owners in an undivided estate following inheritance. The example does not assert ownership percentages or dates.
- **Braindex** is represented as an organisation with legal form BV. **Bink beheer BV** is another organisation, also with legal form BV. The supplied ownership relation is interpreted as a shareholder role towards Braindex. The example does not assert a shareholding percentage or that the shareholder directly owns Braindex's business assets.

The green legal-form nodes are classification values, not Agents or role players. Bink beheer BV is a concrete organisation that can fulfil a role; BV is the legal form associated with it. The same BV value is shared by both organisations without merging those organisations or their ownership relationships.

The ownership arrows are a graphical abbreviation. In the existing pattern, each owner has a RoleFulfillment with `hasPlayer` pointing to that Agent, `fulfillsRole` pointing to its particular contextual role, and `hasPeriod` pointing to its interval. The role's `hasContext` points to the relevant organisation/enterprise. These fulfilments and periods are omitted from this simple view. No new direct object property named after the Dutch arrow labels is introduced. The distinction between enterprise ownership and shareholding is now implemented through separate Role subclasses. Detailed eligibility, percentages, time evaluation and any universal ownership requirement remain open. The drawing labels are not new direct ownership object properties.

Source context for the exceptional sole-proprietorship case: [KVK Gegevenscatalogus 3.0.4h, April 2022, page 9](https://production-site-nl.kvk.bloomreach.cloud/binaries/content/assets/kvkwebsite-nl/categorie/producten-bestellen/gegevenscatalogus.pdf#page=9), and the current [KVK API test scenarios](https://developers.kvk.nl/documentation/testing/swagger-vestigingen-api), which include “Eenmanszaak Met Meerdere Eigenaren”. Staffing is independent of the ownership roles and is not shown.

Validation of the consolidation: the expanded RDF example is checked together with the other synthetic examples and the complete imported model by the normal quality suite. All three example graphs passed OWL 2 DL, consistency and named-class satisfiability checks and the 37 actor/role controls. Both updated diagrams were rendered and visually inspected.
