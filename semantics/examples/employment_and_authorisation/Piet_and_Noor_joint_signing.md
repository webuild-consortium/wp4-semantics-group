# Piet and Noor sign the same document

Piet and Noor are each appointed as a mandatary. The signing rule requires both named people to sign the same document. They may sign at different times. The rule has one outcome for that document.

| Signatures recorded for the document | Signing condition |
| --- | --- |
| Neither signature | Pending |
| Piet's signature only | Pending |
| Noor's signature only | Pending |
| Both Piet's and Noor's signatures | Satisfied |

Each person is responsible for their own signature. Two signatures by Piet would still leave Noor's signature outstanding. Any responsibility for the content of the decision would need a separate basis.

The pair may also be described as a `foaf:Group`. The appointments name the two individuals. Describing the group identifies them together and gives the group the inferred types `foaf:Agent` and `dcterms:Agent`. The appointments and the signing rule supply the facts about who must sign. Whether the group qualifies as an `ebwv:Agent` in its own right is a separate question. [Imported FOAF class module](../../imports/foaf/foaf-classes-2026-11-26.ttl)
